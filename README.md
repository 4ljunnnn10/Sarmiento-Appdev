## My 5 Favorite series
1. Deathnote
2. Naruto
3. Bleach
4. Knock-out

### Deathnote
*Japanese manga series written by Tsugumi Ohba and illustrated by Takeshi Obata, following a high school genius who uses a supernatural notebook to kill criminals as a vigilante named Kira.*

**Notable quote**
> "If we catch Kira, he is evil. If he rules the world, he is justice."

### Naruto

*Naruto is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Masashi Kishimoto that follows the journey of Naruto Uzumaki, a young, socially isolated ninja striving to become the Hokage (the leader of his village).*

**Notable quote**
> "Reflects on tactical realities ("Even the strongest of opponents always has a weakness"), self-acceptance, and how subjective perception defines reality."

### Bleach

*Bleach is a Japanese supernatural shōnen anime series based on the manga by Tite Kubo.

**Notable quote**
> "I wish I could live life five times over. Then I'd be born in five different places... and then, for those five times... I'd fall in love with the same person."

### Knock-out

*Knock Out, internationally and originally known as Hajime no Ippo (The First Step), is a critically acclaimed sports anime and manga series about professional boxing created by George Morikawa.*

**Notable quote**
> "Run until you can't run anymore, and then run again. Constant effort is life's greatest shortcut."

### Baki

*Baki the Grappler franchise is a high-octane martial arts anime and manga series created by Keisuke Itagaki. It follows teenager Baki Hanma as he trains to surpass his father, Yujiro Hanma—known as the "Strongest Creature on Earth".*

**Notable quote**
> "If one is born male, at least once in his lifetime, he'll dream of becoming the strongest man alive."
